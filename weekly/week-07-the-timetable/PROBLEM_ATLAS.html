The Timetable

A city bus and light-rail agency reconciles realtime trip reports with weekly GTFS schedule versions. Build one trustworthy row per trip across service days, midnight boundaries, late files and changing timetable rules.

Output objective: One row per trip with service_date, trip_id, route_id, scheduled_start, actual_start, delay_s, vehicle_id, status and unscheduled.
