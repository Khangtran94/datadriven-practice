THE CHALLENGE · WEEK 08

The Auction

SQL / DAG · AD INVENTORY & BILLING

A search engine sells ad slots at auction. Advertisers pay only for clicks, while the inventory team needs one clean row per ad shown. The nightly DAG reconciles the ad server's event log with billing's account snapshots to produce ad_impressions for 2026-10-09 through 2026-11-07. The log combines serve, click and fraud-verdict events from independent writers; account snapshots provide the advertiser's time zone and currency.

DATA SOURCEad_events + accounts → event history + account history → ad_impressions
1Normalize event identity, time, type, slot and price
2Preserve account and event history across nightly loads
3Settle clicks and fraud verdicts into one impression row
