THE CHALLENGE · WEEK 09

The Basket

SQL / DAG · GROCERY DELIVERY

A customer orders groceries in an app, a shopper scans picked items into bags, and a driver delivers them. Finance needs one row per order with final status, delivery time, charged item count, subtotal and tip. Three feeds disagree in grain and timing: order lifecycle events, shopper scan batches and nightly price snapshots.

DATA SOURCEorder_events · scans · prices → order_charges
1Capture expiring events and retain history
2Replace corrected scan days; deduplicate replays
3Price each order as of placement
